Assignment 45: Storage Comparison

            
Comparison of localStorage and sessionStorage.


            

                

                    
                        

                            Feature
                            localStorage
                            sessionStorage
                        

                    
                    
                        

                            Lifetime
                            Until cleared
                            Usually until the tab session ends
                        

                        

                            Scope
                            Same origin
                            Same origin and tab session
                        

                        

                            Data format
                            Strings
                            Strings
                        

                        

                            Common use
                            Saved preferences
                            Temporary tab data
                        

                    
                

            


            

                Note: Both storage methods are synchronous. Do not store passwords
                or other sensitive information in them.
